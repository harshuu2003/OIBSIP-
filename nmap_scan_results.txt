## 6. Security Observations

- Port 135 – Windows RPC: Restrict unnecessary RPC access to trusted networks.
- Port 139 – NetBIOS: Disable if not required and restrict network exposure.
- Port 445 – SMB: Keep SMB updated and restrict file-sharing access. Disable SMBv1 if enabled.
- Port 3306 – MySQL: Restrict database access to trusted hosts and use strong authentication.
- Port 7070 – Unidentified service: Identify the application and restrict access until its purpose is confirmed.
- Port 6646 – Inconsistent observation: Initially open, later closed; investigate if it reappears.

## 7. Conclusion

The Nmap scan identified multiple open TCP ports on the local Windows machine. Several services were identified, while port 7070 requires further investigation. Port 6646 showed inconsistent scan results and was closed during verification.

Open ports do not automatically indicate vulnerabilities. Further configuration and patch-level checks are needed to determine actual security exposure.
